Maths Formulas

➕ Addition

Addition combines two or more numbers.

a + b = c
  • a and b are numbers being added.
  • c is the sum.
Example:
7 + 5 = 12

➖ Subtraction

Subtraction finds the difference between numbers.

a − b = c
  • a is the minuend.
  • b is the number being subtracted.
  • c is the difference.
Example:
15 − 6 = 9

🍕 Fractions

A fraction represents part of a whole.

Fraction = Numerator / Denominator
  • Numerator = top number.
  • Denominator = bottom number.
  • Denominator cannot be zero.
Example:
3/4 means 3 parts out of 4.

➕ Adding Fractions

a/b + c/d = (ad + bc) / bd
Example:

1/2 + 1/4
= 2/4 + 1/4
= 3/4

➖ Subtracting Fractions

a/b − c/d = (ad − bc) / bd
Example:

3/4 − 1/4
= 2/4 = 1/2

🔄 Equivalent Fractions

a/b = (a × n)/(b × n)
Example:

1/2 = 2/4 = 3/6 = 4/8
